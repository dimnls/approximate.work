Podnap · privacy

Privacy Policy

Podnap does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Last updated October 30, 2026.

No accounts, no tracking

Podnap has no sign-in and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code.

Your podcasts stay on your device

The podcasts you add and your sleep timer settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

To work, Podnap connects directly to:

  • the podcast feeds and audio files you choose to add, hosted by each podcast's publisher;
  • Apple's public iTunes Search API, when you search for a podcast.

These services receive the standard information any internet request includes, such as your IP address. They're governed by their own privacy policies.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Podnap with Siri, Apple handles your voice request under Apple's privacy policy. Podnap only receives which podcast to play.

Feedback emails

If you choose to email feedback, I receive your email address and what you write, plus the app and iOS version the app adds to the message. I use it only to reply and to improve Podnap, and never share it.

Children

Podnap doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
