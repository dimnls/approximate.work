Dense · privacy

Privacy Policy

Your events and reminders never leave your iPhone. Dense sends anonymous usage counts, like which widgets you use, and nothing that identifies you.

Last updated November 14, 2026.

Your calendars and reminders

Dense reads your calendars and reminders through iOS, only after you allow it, to show them in its widgets. It reads them on your iPhone and never sends them anywhere: not your events, not your reminders, not the names of your calendars or lists. Completing a reminder from a widget changes it in Apple's Reminders app, as if you'd done it there.

Your Dense settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which widgets and settings people actually use, Dense sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. These include:

  • which Dense widgets are on your Home Screen and in which sizes;
  • the settings you've chosen in Dense, such as theme, font and text size;
  • when the app is opened;
  • general device information: iPhone model, iOS and app version, language, region and accessibility settings like text size.

TelemetryDeck receives a hashed identifier instead of any ID from your iPhone, so the signals can't be traced back to you or linked across apps. It doesn't store IP addresses. The signals never include your events, reminders, or calendar and list names. Dense has no advertising and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

No accounts

Dense has no sign-in and doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Opening other apps

If you choose another calendar app in Open In, tapping a widget opens that app at the right day. Only the date is passed along.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, I receive your email address and what you write, plus details the app adds to help fix problems: the app and iOS version, iPhone model, whether Dense can access Calendar and Reminders, how many calendars you show, and your Dense settings. Never your events or reminders. I use it only to reply and to improve Dense, and never share it.

Beta testing

If you test Dense through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight with me, under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Dense doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
